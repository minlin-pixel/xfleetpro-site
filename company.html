Built to make Geotab work in the real world.

xFleetPro is a services and engineering partner for fleets running on Geotab. We help you go from “we have the data” to “we run the business with it”—with clean configuration, governed analytics, and integrations that stick.

What we do

Geotab implementation, optimization, data modeling, Power BI dashboards, and API integrations—delivered with clear scope and an operator-first mindset.

Who we serve

Vocational, field service, public administration, and transportation fleets that need safety, uptime, and accountability.

How we work

Short feedback loops, shared KPI definitions, secure-by-default access, and a steady improvement cadence after go-live.

Our approach

Telematics success is rarely a “feature” problem—it’s a consistency problem. We focus on the fundamentals that reduce noise and increase trust.

  • Standardized naming for assets, groups, and zones
  • Rules and exceptions tuned for your actual operating conditions
  • Role-based dashboards with agreed-upon KPI definitions
  • Integrations that eliminate manual re-entry and spreadsheet drift
